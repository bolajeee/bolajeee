## About me

I work across the whole lifecycle of a product: the screens people use, the APIs and data underneath, and the pipelines and cloud services that ship it and keep it running. Most of my work is for real clients and early-stage products in civic tech, ed-tech, insurance, agriculture and e-commerce.

- 💼 Software Engineer at **Leaders Network**: backend services, system design and delivery
- 🏗️ Building **CivicAI**, an AI-driven citizen reporting platform, and **Furrow**, a home-growing app for busy households
- 📚 Currently deepening infrastructure and operations in **TS Academy's DevOps Engineering programme**
- 🧪 I came to software from food and bioprocess engineering, and I still treat products as systems
